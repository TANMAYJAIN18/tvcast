Let's connect your TV

Three quick steps, only once. After that it's one click to play.
1
Turn on ADB debugging

Fire TV: Settings → My Fire TV → About → click the name 7×, then Developer Options → ADB Debugging ON.
Android TV: Settings → About → click Build 7×, then Developer options → USB/Network debugging ON.

2
Find your TV

Same Wi-Fi as this computer. When the TV asks “Allow USB debugging?”, tick Always allow → OK.

3
Press play

TV Cast installs and tunes Kodi on the TV for you, then plays movies at full quality.
